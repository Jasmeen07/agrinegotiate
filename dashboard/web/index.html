AgriNegotiate benchmark · results as of

Do learning agents treat farmers fairly when they negotiate, and can a platform rule fix it?

Farmer and buyer agents bargain over a price in a simulated market. We measure how the gains from each deal are split, compare against game-theory reference points, and test a platform fee on buyers who underpay.

Simulation results. Not tested with real farmers or traders. Prices are shown as a share of the market reference price.
Finding 1 · exploitation

How the gains are split without any fairness rule

Each dot is one independently trained pair of agents. The position is the farmer's share of the gains from trade. The bar shows the interquartile mean across runs with its 95% interval.

Finding 2 · fairness mechanism · perishable crop

Efficiency against what farmers capture, for each platform rule

Up and to the right is better: more of the possible gains realised, and more of them reaching farmers. Each point is one rule at one strength, averaged over training runs.

Follow-up · exploratory, 3 to 5 runs per point · buyer values vary widely

The same rules when buyers differ a lot in what the crop is worth to them

Here a price floor has to shut out buyers who value the crop less, so it loses trades. This experiment was added after seeing the result above, so treat it as a lead to confirm, not a finding.

Finding 3 · intermittent links

What a poor connection costs, and whether delay-aware agents recover it

The farmer's link is up for the stated share of the time. Delay-aware agents have memory and can see message age, offer validity and their own link state.

How to read the numbers
TermMeaning
Gains from tradeWhat the pair gains by trading with each other instead of taking their fallbacks.
Efficiency (E)Share of the maximum possible gains that were realised. Negative means agents waited, lost value, and ended worse than walking away.
Farmer gain capture (FGC)Share of the maximum possible gains that reached farmers, counting unsold lots.
Farmer shareFarmers' part of the realised gains. 0.5 is the Nash bargaining split.
AgreementDeals closed out of the deals in the best possible matching.
